02Featured projects

Our creations

Three things we are making right now. Pick one.

Cubamii

A calm 3D logic puzzle for Android. The numbers on every face tell you how many cubes of each row belong to the animal. Chisel away the rest, paint what is safe, and a voxel animal slowly appears.

  • 84voxel animals to uncover
  • 7³–10³puzzle cubes, from easy to hard
  • 3tools: chisel, paint, inspect

Katsumii

An offline trading journal. Your trades, your machine. See net P&L, win rate, profit factor and a daily heatmap at a glance, across funded, challenge, personal and backtest accounts.

Visit katsumii.com ↗
  • Offlineyour data stays on your machine
  • 4modes: funded, challenge, personal, backtest
  • DailyP&L heatmap and edge drivers

Media Center In development

Your own library of films, series, music, radio, live TV and podcasts in one calm, fast interface. Early screens, the name may still change.

  • 6sections: films, series, music, radio, live TV, podcasts
  • Yoursbuilt around your own library
