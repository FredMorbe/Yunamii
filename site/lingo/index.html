Learn on an island

Concrete words arrive as pictures, every other word on a small card with an example. Then you build sentences from words you already know. The course is a painted island: each part a signboard on a winding path, each chapter a stop along the way.

  • 9 chaptersof Mandarin and of Japanese, five of Korean so far
  • Level quizstarts you where you really are, or at the first word if you are new
  • Reading aidPinyin or Zhuyin, Rōmaji or furigana, Romaja, or none

Practice that feels like play

Short games keep words fresh: a speed round, a match round, paper lanterns that rise over a field of characters, a tone swipe for Mandarin. Review brings back the words that are due, and the dictionary finds a word by its script, its reading or its meaning, even if all you can do is draw the character.

  • Spaced reviewwords come back after 1, 2, 4, 8 … days until they stay
  • Dictionarysearch by script, reading or meaning, or write with your finger
  • Word bookevery word grows as a plant, from seed to blossom

A buddy that keeps you going

Pick a daily goal from 10 to 50 XP and watch the ring fill. Keep a streak, with freezes for the days life gets in the way, finish three quests for a chest, and collect medals and seals as you go. A gentle reminder if you want one, and nothing shouty.

  • 60 levelsfrom the first word to sentence builder and beyond
  • 88 medalsin 25 ladders, for habits as much as for scores
  • 2 freezescover the days you cannot practise
