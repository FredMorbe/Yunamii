Think in three dimensions

A flat nonogram is a grid. Here you solve a whole block: turn it, look at it from every side, peel off single layers. Clues along three axes tell you where your next safe move is.

  • Three toolschisel what is empty, paint what is sure, inspect a cube’s rows
  • X-ray and peellook through the block or take it apart layer by layer
  • Hints and undoa way out whenever you get stuck

From the first animal to a master puzzle

Start with a short guided tutorial, then solve small puzzles and discover more animals. Larger models are assembled from several puzzle cubes. Landmarks, vehicles and whole scenes grow your collection as you go.

  • Four collectionsanimals, landmarks, vehicles and dioramas
  • Daily and weeklya new puzzle every day, a bigger challenge every week
  • Your paceplay normally, or choose stricter rules and time challenges

Every model is a small sculpture

A solved puzzle is not a tick on a list. It is a model you can turn, zoom into and share as a picture. Colour themes, light and dark appearance, text size and left-handed controls help you make the app your own.

  • Turn and zoomlook at your model from every side
  • Sharesend a solved model as a picture
  • Light and darkwith colour themes to choose from
